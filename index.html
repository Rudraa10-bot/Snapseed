About SnapSeed

SnapSeed is a simple personal expense management application designed to help users track their daily expenses, manage monthly budgets and monitor spending across different categories. Users can upload receipts or manually add expenses to keep an accurate record of their finances. This project is built as part of a Full Stack Development course.
